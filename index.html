Employee Cost Tracker

A Windows desktop app for companies to record and review the daily expenses of their employees.

What it does

  • Employees enter their daily expenses (travel, food, fuel and more) until the day's cut-off time.
  • Admins review, correct and export the expenses of their organisation.
  • Every day the app can save a spreadsheet of that day's expenses to the company's own Google Drive.

Where the data lives

The app runs on a computer of the company that uses it. All data is stored there, encrypted. The developer does not run a server and does not receive any of the data.

Google Drive

When an admin connects Google Drive, the app asks only for the drive.file permission: it can see and change only the files it creates itself (a folder named "Employee Cost Tracker" with one spreadsheet per day). It cannot read any other file in the Drive.
