Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who is responsible for the data

Employee Cost Tracker is installed and run by a company on its own computer. That company decides who uses the app and is responsible for the data entered in it. The developer of the app does not run a server, does not collect usage data and has no access to the data.

Data the app stores

  • Account details of the people who use it: name, email address, optional phone number, role and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense entries: date, time, category, amount and optional place and notes.
  • An audit log of changes, including the computer's network address.

Everything is stored on the company's computer in an encrypted database. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party, except the Google Drive export described below, and password-reset emails that the company's own mail server sends when someone asks for a reset code.

Google user data

  • What is accessed: when an admin connects Google Drive, the app asks for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission only. It can create, see and update only the files it created itself: a folder named "Employee Cost Tracker" and one spreadsheet of expenses per day. It cannot see any other file in the Drive. It also reads the email address of the connected Google account to show which account is connected.
  • How it is used: only to write the daily expense spreadsheets of the company into that company's own Drive.
  • Storage: the tokens from Google are stored encrypted on the company's computer. They are never sent anywhere else.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not shared with, sold or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train any AI model.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

An admin can disconnect Google Drive in the app at any time, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The spreadsheets already in the Drive stay there until the owner deletes them. Data on the company's computer is deleted by the company, for example by deleting a client in the app or uninstalling the app and choosing to delete its data.

Contact

For questions about your data, contact the company that gave you access to the app. Questions about the app itself: open an issue.
